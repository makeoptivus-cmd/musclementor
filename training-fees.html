Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about our training programs

Can I change my plan later?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes will take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

Is there a money-back guarantee?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied with your training program, we'll provide a full refund within the first 30 days.

Do I need any equipment?

Our programs are designed to be flexible. We offer both gym-based and home workout options. For home workouts, minimal equipment like dumbbells or resistance bands may be recommended.

How long are the courses?

Course lengths vary from 6 to 16 weeks depending on the program. Each course is self-paced, so you can complete it according to your schedule.

What if I'm a complete beginner?

Our Foundation Fitness course is perfect for beginners. It covers all the basics of exercise, proper form, and safety guidelines to help you start your fitness journey confidently.
