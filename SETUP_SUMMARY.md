## 💡 Image Tips

### Achievement Image
- ✅ Use landscape orientation (wider than tall)
- ✅ Show team, awards, or success moments
- ✅ High quality, well-lit photo
- ✅ Keep under 500KB file size

### Certificate Images
- ✅ Use portrait orientation (taller than wide)
- ✅ Scan at 300 DPI minimum
- ✅ Ensure text is readable
- ✅ Keep each under 300KB
- ✅ Use consistent sizes
